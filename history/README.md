# Historical material

These files document preliminary, superseded, or pre-numbered development. They are not additional validated final study results and should not replace the final outputs indexed by EXP001–EXP015.

- `pre_numbered/`: earlier SMAP/MSL and Round2 source, results, and audits. Unrelated sweep weights/data and private correspondence were deliberately excluded.
- `EXP005_command_aware_TCN_bundle/`: original CPU tuning protocol/source capsule.
- `EXP006_tuned_GCN_bundle/` and `EXP006_cuda_amendment_bundle/`: CPU planning and CPU-to-CUDA migration; final GPU source/results are under EXP006.
- `EXP007_original_input/`: pre-E01 missing-import capsule. Final EXP007 used the E01 capsule under its own experiment directory.
- `EXP013_superseded_capsules/`: original V1, E01, E01 P01, E02 SIMPLE, and E02 performance-check ZIPs, kept in the companion files with their original names. These are superseded engineering/source versions, not compatible replacements for final E03 states.
- `early_anomaly_freeze/` and `research_decisions/`: historical detector freeze and the documented transition to the retrospective benchmark. They do not reopen selection or supersede final results.

Source capsule folders named `e02_evidence`, `e03_evidence`, `SIMPLE_E2E`, `FICTIONAL`, or synthetic tests contain engineering fixtures. Their numbers/timings are not NASA scientific runs. The latest report source in `report/` takes precedence over older report snippets retained in analysis packages. The separate runtime appendix proposal is supporting audit material, not a replacement for the user's latest appendix.
