# E03 production amendment

E03 retains the E02 runner, checkpoint format, automatic mounted-Drive backup implementation, score cache and scientific pipeline. The single mathematical correction expresses the four valid dilated encoder convolutions (dilations 2, 4, 8, 16) as causal tap windows and FP32 GEMM. Dilation 1 stays native. The primitive is identical to the graph correction validated on A100 in the preceding benchmark. It is now enabled for graph, message-removal and direct-fusion roles because all three share this encoder. It covers command and telemetry histories. No command caching, branch removal, reduced receptive field or altered architecture is introduced. The frozen `CommandReferenceGCN.forward` AST is identical; convolution parameters, constructor RNG order and state-dict keys are unchanged. GDN/CATCH source, configuration, losses, optimizers and schedules are unchanged.

## Numerical acceptance and reproducibility

The native source is preserved with SHA-256 `de8c5697f92edb0f1bc35e875bcd29a0f9b0fe1dd02d2f3225dc86057b4e54e4`. The CPU suite compares all three roles, both mission widths and seeds 17/29/43 for two Adam updates, including inactive/null command support. It checks FP64 kernel tap order and derivatives, gradcheck, eval outputs/input derivatives, exact initialisation and RNG, native warm-Adam comparisons and exact corrected warm-checkpoint replay for every role. A builder attempt to compare a full 256-sample TRAIN batch on CPU terminated with exit 137 (SIGKILL) before producing a comparison; its cause was not established. It is recorded as NOT COMPLETED, not a pass and not GPU evidence. No reduced batch replaces the actual Colab full-batch check. The retained scientific/runner tests and fictional 22-fit integration exercise stage ordering, restoration, label blocking and final evaluation.

FP32 reduction order changes. Graph-only P02 used parameter atol 2e-6 and Adam moment atol 2e-9. Applying the same primitive to fusion produced an initial parameter difference of 2.72924e-6; the second update produced a moment difference of 5.90444e-9. Both are recorded, including the initial failed acceptance result. Near-zero gradients relative to Adam epsilon amplify small gradient differences. E03 explicitly adopts parameter atol **5e-6**, rtol **2e-5**, and moment atol **2e-8**, rtol **2e-4**. The first-moment absolute allowance is (1−beta1) times the gradient absolute allowance. Parameter atol is 0.5% of the frozen 0.001 learning rate. These are disclosed engineering equivalence tolerances, not altered losses, detector gates, scientific acceptance rules or a claim of equal scientific results. They do not establish long-trajectory equality.

Output/loss tolerances remain atol **2e-6**, rtol **2e-5**; gradient tolerances remain atol **2e-7**, rtol **2e-4**. Every optimizer update is additionally checked against an independent FP64 calculation of ordinary Adam from that run's FP32 moments and pre-update parameters (atol 3e-8, rtol 3e-7). All parameter/gradient keys, including `None`, are checked. Adam counters, synthetic payloads and Python/NumPy/Torch/CUDA RNG must be exact. Corrected replay must be bitwise exact. The Colab hardware check applies the same fixed tolerances to two full-batch updates for each of the six corrected role/mission combinations. It records every tensor's errors; no tolerance is adjusted at execution to obtain a pass.

## Deadline estimate

Using approximate 56 s native graph compute divided by the reported 501.39 gives 0.11169 s/batch for the measured computational portion. This is an inferred absolute time, not a new measurement. At 25 epochs, MSL's 127 FIT batches cost approximately 5.91 minutes per fit; its nine new-model fits cost approximately 53.2 minutes if removed/fusion share that speed. SMAP has 310 FIT batches. With the unmeasured assumption of 24/54 command-work scaling, its nine fits cost approximately 57.7 minutes; with identical batch time they cost approximately 129.8 minutes. The **18-fit FIT compute subtotal is 1.85–3.05 hours** under these assumptions. Early stopping may reduce it. Full runtime can exceed this range; role speedups, host/data overhead and SMAP scaling are unmeasured on A100 until section 4.

The four unchanged baseline epoch caps contain **110,040 GDN FIT steps + 36,840 STOP batches** and **6,835 CATCH FIT steps + 2,620 STOP batches**. Each extra 10 ms/GDN FIT step adds 18.34 minutes; each extra 1 s/CATCH FIT step adds 1.90 hours. These are workload sensitivities, not measured baseline speeds. Selection, confirmation, full TEST export and Drive copies are additional; the stopwatch for the first real epoch and full-batch checks supplies an immediate practical forecast. MSL CATCH has 210,881,152 parameters; SMAP 53,464,208. Dense FP32 parameter/gradient/two-moment estimates are 3.14/0.797 GiB, not measured peaks or guaranteed bounds. Activations and checkpoint restoration/host RAM can dominate. Neither 40GB feasibility nor completion tonight is assumed.
